What we deliver

There are no standard packages. We design each project around your product, your target country and your decision.

01

Landed Cost Diagnostic

The full cost of one product in one country, from factory to shelf. We compare cross-border sales, a local distributor and local stock, and the shelf price each model allows.

02

Market Entry Plan

Import rules, product certification (for example SEC in Chile, INMETRO in Brazil), sales channel, local partners and a step-by-step plan.

03

Freight & Logistics Strategy

Routes, ports, Incoterms, freight tenders and dangerous goods, including lithium batteries (IMDG Class 9).

04

Risk Radar

Continuous monitoring of geopolitics, freight rates and regulation that affect your trade lanes, with early alerts.
